Aligned Mars layers for QGIS

1. Add MOLA_terreno.tif, TES_inercia_termica.tif and Marte_nombres.shp.
2. Right-click MOLA_terreno and select Set Project CRS from Layer.
3. Set MOLA_terreno to Hillshade with band 1 (elevation). Set TES_inercia_termica
   to Singleband pseudocolor on band 1 and lower its opacity to show the relief.
   Label Marte_nombres with the name field. Keep the shapefile files together.
4. TES_relieve.tif is the georeferenced ASU color preview with shaded relief.

The rasters share one Mars equirectangular CRS, extent, and 2880 x 1440 grid.
The map spans -180 to 180 degrees east and uses planetocentric latitude.
The numeric TES layer uses J m^-2 K^-1 s^-1/2. The original values 25 and 602
match the grayscale-only cells of the ASU preview after a 180-degree longitude
shift and are shown as NoData. The remaining numeric TES values are preserved.

The processed MOLA elevation, slope, and roughness bands were averaged onto the
native TES grid (8 pixels/degree, ~7.41 km at the equator). The source MOLA band
values in the original GeoTIFF remain available at its original resolution.
